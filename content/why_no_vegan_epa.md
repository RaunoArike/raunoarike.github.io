## While this problem hasn't been solved, what should you do in the meantime?

The best option is probably to either eat anchovies and sardines or to buy supplements based on them - see https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/MvXbFB2Hhgq46toye/a-vegan-case-for-eating-sardines-and-anchovies. It seems plausible that this would be the morally better choice even if the current vegan omega 3 supplements contained adequate amounts of EPA, given that it isn't clear whether eating anchovies and sardines is even net-negative from a welfare standpoint (pros: anchovies and sardines are small and mostly exhibit simple, instinct-driven behaviours (see the post linked above, ctrl+F "instinct"), we only eat wild-caught sardines and anchovies, "Sardines and anchovies lay tens to hundreds of thousands of eggs each year, yet fewer than one in a thousand survive to adulthood. When they do, most ultimately die through predation, starvation, or disease. [...] Once caught, they are typically swallowed whole and remain alive in the predator's stomach for around 20 minutes, during which they suffocate or are slowly digested by acids and enzymes." (from the post linked above); cons: there are annually enforced catch quotas for sardines and anchovies, so if many vegans start eating them, prices will rise and other people might choose less ethical options then (though also consider that it might simply be that fewer sardines and anchovies would be used as pet food in that case; see https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/MvXbFB2Hhgq46toye/a-vegan-case-for-eating-sardines-and-anchovies?commentId=onE3ZAy6HmzJoJDnf and the thread below), since sardines and anchovies are small, we eat many more of them than we would eat e.g. salmon). Even if you consider it somewhat net-negative to consume sardines and anchovies, the supplements based on them are much cheaper than vegan ones and you can donate the difference to animal welfare charities (though consider that buying vegan ones and thereby raising the demand for algae-based supplements increases demand for them, which might be an effective way to solve the civilizational inadequacy; also consider that fish-based supplements might have heavy metal issues). In case you decide to go for vegan capsules, https://www.vegetology.com/supplements/omega-3 has the biggest EPA content out of all the options I've seen, as well as good shipping coverage.
