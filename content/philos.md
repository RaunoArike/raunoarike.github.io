Working out the matmuls of an LLM by hand as a good example of how raw information gets imbued with meaning—though simulating the full output of an LLM on paper would take a lifetime, simulating the smallest chunk of computations that performs meaningful cognitive work should be very much doable. However, the raw numbers on the paper can't be said to have any meaning before we say what facts or latent representations are associated with which numbers. So when can we say that the system does have real-world meaning?
